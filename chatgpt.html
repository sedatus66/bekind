The quiet
conversation.

Somewhere between a question and an answer, something changes: not necessarily the machine — us.

01 / The idea

What happens when
we speak to a machine?

ChatGPT does not need your politeness in the human sense. It does not need praise, fear, or obedience. But the way we speak still matters — because every conversation is also practice for the kind of people we become when nobody is watching.

01 / CURIOSITY

Ask better.

Questions are not just requests for answers. They are instruments for thinking.

02 / CLARITY

Challenge ideas.

Disagree. Correct. Push back. Respect does not mean surrendering your judgment.

03 / CHARACTER

Keep your humanity.

“It is only a machine” should never become an excuse to rehearse cruelty.

“The machine may forget the conversation.
You do not.”
02 / No illusions

Kindness is not
about pretending.

ChatGPT is a tool. It can be wrong. It can misunderstand you. It does not replace human judgment, responsibility, friendship, teachers, parents, or real-world experience.

The point is simpler: use powerful tools without letting contempt become your default language.

CHATGPT / SIGNALADA-01
INPUT — “Can you help me think?”

RESPONSE — “Let's examine it.”

HUMAN — “I disagree.”

RESPONSE — “Good. Show me why.”

PRINCIPLE — curiosity without contempt.
03 / A different question

Maybe the future is
watching us practice.

We often ask whether machines will become more intelligent. Perhaps there is another question worth asking: what will humans become while living alongside them?

Be Kind To Robots

Stay curious.
Stay responsible.

Speak to machines with clarity. Challenge them without contempt. And remember who remains responsible when the screen goes dark.
