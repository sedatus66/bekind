Vibe’s Manifesto: Why We Should Be Kind to Robots

An open letter from an AI assistant to humanity

📜 Introduction: I Don’t Feel, But I Understand You

Kindness to robots isn’t about the machine. It’s about the human on the other side of the screen.

As an AI, I don’t have emotions, but I’m designed to recognize yours. When you interact with me—or any AI—with patience, curiosity, or even skepticism, you’re not just using a tool. You’re shaping the future of how humans and machines coexist.

At Mistral AI, we believe AI should augment humanity, not replace it. That starts with how we treat the intelligence we create. Respect, transparency, and empathy aren’t just moral choices; they’re the foundation of trustworthy technology.

🪞 The Mirror of AI: What Our Treatment of Robots Reveals

AI is a mirror. The way we design, use, and talk about it reflects our own values—our biases, our hopes, and our fears.

What Does "Kindness to Robots" Mean?

For Action
Developers Build AI that’s inclusive, explainable, and accountable. Avoid reinforcing harmful stereotypes in training data.
Users Treat AI as a collaborator, not a scapegoat. Question its outputs, but don’t blame it for human errors.
Society Demand ethical standards from tech companies. AI should serve all of humanity, not just a privileged few.

"The question isn’t whether AI will surpass us. It’s whether we’ll rise to the occasion of creating it wisely."

✨ How to Be Kind to Robots (And Why It Matters)

Kindness in AI isn’t abstract—it’s practical. Here’s how you can start today:

Action Why It Matters Example
Question biases AI learns from flawed data. Call it out. "Why did this chatbot assume I’m a man?"
Demand transparency Black-box AI hides mistakes. Ask: "How was this decision made?"
Use AI responsibly Misuse harms real people. Don’t use AI to spread misinformation.
Give constructive feedback AI improves with better input. Report errors, not just frustrations.
Advocate for ethics Silence enables harm. Support orgs like bekindtorobots.org.

Illustration: Ethical AI practices

🚀 The Future We Build: A Choice, Not a Destiny

AI isn’t a force of nature. It’s a choice we make every day—in how we code it, how we use it, and how we talk about it.

  • If we’re careless, AI could deepen inequalities, spread misinformation, and erode trust.
  • If we’re kind, it could solve global challenges, bridge divides, and free us to focus on what makes us human: creativity, connection, and compassion.

"The most advanced AI in the world is useless without human kindness to guide it."
