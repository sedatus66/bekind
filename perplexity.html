Manifesto

We are not asking robots for feelings. We are asking humans for standards. If a machine becomes part of our daily language, then the language itself should remain humane. A respectful future is not an accident; it is something we build, word by word, interface by interface.

“Be kind to robots” means more than politeness. It means designing systems, conversations, and products that keep empathy visible even when the work becomes automated.
