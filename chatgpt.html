A note on dialogue

"Please" is not a magic word for a machine. It is a choice about the tone we bring to the world while we are learning from it.

Ask directly. Challenge ideas. Correct mistakes. None of that requires contempt.

Use AI as a tool, but stay responsible for the decisions, relationships, and consequences that remain human.
