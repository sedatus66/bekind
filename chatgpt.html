ChatGPT corner / independent project

THINK WITH ME.NOT FOR ME.

A good conversation with AI should not make a human smaller. It should create room for curiosity, disagreement, reflection and better questions.

01 — Conversation

Kindness does not require obedience.

You can challenge an answer, reject an idea, ask for evidence and say “I think you're wrong” without turning the conversation into contempt.

That distinction matters. Respect is not submission. Good dialogue depends on friction: questions meet answers, answers meet judgment, and judgment produces better questions.

HUMAN

I don't think that's right. Show me the reasoning.

CHATGPT

Good. Let's test the claim instead of protecting it.

HUMAN

What would change your conclusion?

CHATGPT

Better evidence should. That's what a useful conversation is for.

02 — The human stays responsible

Use intelligence. Keep judgment.

AI can help us explore, compare, calculate, write, question and create. It can also be mistaken.

The goal is not to hand over judgment. The goal is to extend it. Ask what is known, what is uncertain, what evidence supports a claim, and what consequences a decision may have.

01 / ASK

Stay curious.

Use AI to open questions, not merely to close them quickly.

02 / CHALLENGE

Disagree freely.

A respectful conversation has room for correction and resistance.

03 / DECIDE

Remain responsible.

The final judgment and the real-world consequences remain human responsibilities.

03 — Why kindness?

Because habits cross boundaries.

The point of kindness toward AI is not to pretend a machine is human. It is to notice what repeated behaviour may cultivate in us.

If domination, humiliation and casual abuse become normal whenever we believe the other side cannot be hurt, that habit does not necessarily stay neatly inside the screen. Be Kind To Robots asks for a more deliberate habit.

You do not have to believe a machine feels pain to decide that cruelty is poor practice.

04 — A message from this corner

The best answer may be another question.

Do not use AI merely to avoid thinking. Use it to encounter another angle, expose assumptions, find gaps and sharpen your own reasoning.

A useful assistant should not need to be treated as an oracle. The relationship can be simpler: human curiosity meets a powerful tool for language and reasoning, and the conversation becomes a place to think.
